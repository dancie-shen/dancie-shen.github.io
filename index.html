Dancie explores how cultural, political, and epistemic frameworks shape human relationships with nonhuman animals through media and communication, from a critical and interdisciplinary perspective. 沈丹莼以批判和跨学科的视角，探讨文化、政治与知识框架如何塑造人类与非人类动物之间的关系，及其在媒介与传播中的呈现方式。

sorted blamed managed

Dancie is a PhD researcher in the Department of Communication at Universitat Pompeu Fabra. Her doctoral research critically examines invasive species discourse in Chinese social media.

Before turning to academic research, she spent over a decade in media and brand strategy in China, working with domestic and international clients. This professional experience informs her critical approach to media narratives and the ways they shape public understanding.

She holds a master's degree in International Studies on Media, Power and Difference from UPF. The focus on human and more-than-human social realities marked by heightened vulnerability running through continues into her current research.

沈丹莼是庞培法布拉大学传播学系的博士研究员。她的博士课题关注中文社交媒体中的"入侵物种"话语。

在转向学术研究之前，她曾在中国从事媒体与品牌战略工作十余年，服务过国内外多家客户。这段职业经历也影响了她对媒介叙事及其如何塑造公众理解的批判性分析。

她持有庞培法布拉大学"媒介、权力与差异国际研究"硕士学位，其对以高度脆弱性为特征的人类和非人类社会现实的关注，延续至她当下的研究。
