Dancie explores how cultural, political, and epistemic frameworks shape human relationships with nonhuman animals through media and communication, from a critical and interdisciplinary perspective. 沈丹莼以批判和跨学科的视角，探讨文化、政治与知识框架如何塑造人类与非人类动物之间的关系，及其在媒介与传播中的呈现方式。

Dancie is a PhD researcher in the Department of Communication at Universitat Pompeu Fabra. Her doctoral research examines how invasive species discourse in Chinese-language social media sorts nonhuman animals — and, by extension, people — into categories of belonging and threat.

Before turning to academic research, she spent over a decade in media and brand strategy in China, working with domestic and international clients. That earlier work — building narratives for audiences — now shapes how she reads them critically.

She holds a master's degree in International Studies on Media, Power and Difference at UPF. The thread running through both — who gets to speak, who gets misread, who gets called invasive — continues into her current research.

沈丹莼是庞培法布拉大学传播学系的博士研究员。她的博士课题关注中文社交媒体中的"入侵物种"话语如何将非人类动物——进而延伸至人——划分为"本土"与"威胁"两类。

在转向学术研究之前，她曾在中国从事媒体与品牌战略工作十余年，服务过国内外多家客户。那段"为受众构建叙事"的经历，如今成为她批判性解读叙事的基础。

她持有庞培法布拉大学"媒介、权力与差异国际研究"硕士学位。贯穿其中的主线——谁能发声、谁被误读、谁被称为"入侵者"——延续至她当下的研究。
